# Pickman's Whisper — What's In The Game

## The Blade & The Bond

- Find Pickman's Blade and it becomes a living, whispering thing in your inventory.
- The knife's voice only fully "wakes up" once you've bonded with it — which requires both owning the blade and having the Lady Killer perk. Without Lady Killer, the blade stays a silent knife no matter how long you carry it.

## Hunger

- The blade develops a hunger meter (0–100) that climbs slowly over real game-days, not minutes — expect roughly a day per stage.
- As hunger rises through five stages (Calm → Restless → Hungry → Starving → Desperate), the whispers change tone — from admiration, to jealousy, to open kill-urges — commenting on nearby women.
- Kill with the blade to satiate hunger — always works, regardless of what stage you're in.
- Patience pays off: if you let hunger sit at Desperate for a full 13 in-game hours before finally satiating it, you're rewarded with a temporary +4 Agility / +4 Charisma boost that lasts as long as you stay Calm afterward.

## Noticing & Stalking

- The blade notices non-hostile women around you, with hunger-appropriate ambient whisper lines.
- Aiming/looking at a woman repeatedly escalates recognition — first silent, then a recognition line, then sharper hunger-stage lines.
- Sleeping women get their own special recognition line if you catch them dozing.
- Plenty of women you'll meet are just "Settler" or "Resident" — faceless, forgettable. Pickman's Whisper lets you fix that. Aim at her, open the Victims page in MCM, type a name, and apply it. She'll carry that name from then on — it shows up when you look at her, and the blade uses it in its whispers instead of talking about her like a stranger. Up to 32 women can be named this way.
- A Potential Victims list (MCM) tracks up to 32 women you've marked, with world display names that match what the whispers call them.

## Killing

- Kills made with Pickman's Blade drawn on eligible women trigger special praise — named victims get unique dedicated lines instead of generic ones.
- Execute: aim at a living, eligible woman and hit \ to bring up a menu — Sever Head (requires the blade) or Smash Head In (requires a heavy blunt weapon: baseball bat, sledgehammer, super sledge, pipe wrench, or pool cue). Instant kill, gory finisher, fully counts toward hunger satiation and named-kill voice lines like any blade kill.
- Corpse dismemberment: aim at a dead body with the blade drawn and press / for a limb-selection menu (sever head/arms/legs).

## Necromantic Intimacy (soft integration)

- If you also run the Necromantic mod, named victims get special voice lines at the start and end of those scenes.

## Corpses Decay Over Time

- Kills you make with the blade visibly decay over real game-hours — pallor, bruising, and worse the longer the body sits.
- Bodies reach a "ripe" stage where the blade urges you to eat them (Cannibal perk); doing so grants an Endurance buff and clears them from your victim list.

## Force Trade & Slavery

- A "Force Trade" activate option on eligible living women opens their inventory to loot/gift, with a one-time gear strip the first time.
- Trading her slave gear (or a dedicated "Enslave" option) lets you take her as a collared follower — she travels and teleports with you, is killable (not a vanilla companion), and can be freed via MCM.
- Take Her: with an enslaved victim, trigger a full two-actor AAF scene instead of the old plain "Free" option.

Not all features are documented to not spoil the fun.
