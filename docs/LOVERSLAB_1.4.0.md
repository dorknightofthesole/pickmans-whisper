**Pickman's Whisper 1.4.0 — Cut Off Tits**

New option on the butcher menu.

**How to use**
1. Draw Pickman's Blade.
2. Aim at a dead adult woman.
3. Press `/` for the butcher menu.
4. Choose **Cut Off Tits**.

**What you get**
- Her body is replaced with a custom mutilated body based on **Fusion Girl**.
- A severed **Cut Off Tits** prop drops beside her — it falls to the ground, can be pushed around, and can be looted.

Install with MO2 from `PickmansWhisper-1.4.0.zip`. Quit to desktop after updating so MCM reloads.
